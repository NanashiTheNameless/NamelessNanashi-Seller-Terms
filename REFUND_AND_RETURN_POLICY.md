# Refund and Return Policy

**Effective date:** June 22, 2026

This Refund and Return Policy applies to purchases from **NamelessNanashi**. A written quote, invoice, warranty, or contract controls if it says something different.

## 1. General Rule

We stand behind our work, but we also sell made-to-order hardware and services. Because of that, returns and refunds are limited. Refunds and returns are not guaranteed in any situation unless we expressly agree in writing or a written warranty or law requires a specific remedy.

## 2. Eligible Returns

For standard hardware that is not custom, altered, or a service, we may, at our sole discretion, accept a return if you contact us within 14 days of delivery, the item is unused and resellable, and you get return authorization before sending it back.

If approved, refunds are usually issued to the original payment method minus any restocking fee, shipping charges, non-recoverable payment fees, and any loss caused by damage or missing parts, to the extent allowed by law.

## 3. Non-Returnable Items

These are final sale unless we say otherwise in writing or a written warranty applies:

- Custom-built or custom-modified hardware
- OpenShock-related custom hardware or assemblies made to your specs
- Anything built, flashed, configured, engraved, or altered for you
- Software, firmware, downloads, code, scripts, or configuration files
- Consulting, coding-as-a-service, debugging, integration work, or other labor
- Opened, installed, used, damaged, clearance, or final-sale items
- Items sent back without authorization or with missing parts

## 4. Services and Code Work

All coding, debugging, consulting, firmware, integration, and other service work is non-refundable once work begins. If a project is quoted by milestone, you pay for the work completed through the cancellation date.

If we agree to a partial refund for a service engagement, the refund may be reduced by time spent, non-recoverable third-party fees, delivered work, rush fees, and expenses already incurred.

## 5. Defective or Wrong Item Claims

If you receive something defective or materially different from what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos or video.

If we confirm the issue, we may choose repair, replacement, replacement parts, store credit, partial refund, or full refund, to the extent allowed by law. No remedy is guaranteed.

We are not responsible for damage caused by misuse, improper installation, modification, improper power, accidents, liquid damage, wear and tear, or unauthorized repair.

## 6. Restocking Fee

We may charge a restocking fee of up to 20% on approved returns for non-defective items.

## 7. Return Shipping

Unless we made the error or the item is defective under an applicable warranty, you pay return shipping, insurance, and any risk of loss during return transit. If we provide a prepaid label, the cost may be deducted from your refund where allowed by law.

## 8. Shipping Charges

Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or law requires a shipping refund.

## 9. Refund Timing

Approved refunds are usually processed after we receive and inspect the returned item or confirm the service cancellation amount. Bank or card timing is outside our control.

## 10. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Before filing a chargeback or payment dispute, contact us so we can try to resolve it. If you file one without contacting us first, we may submit proof of delivery, communication records, project notes, photos, and other evidence to contest it.

## 11. Warranty Claims

If a product includes a separate written warranty, that warranty controls the remedy process. This Policy does not add any warranty beyond what we give in writing.

To request return authorization, contact:

We may update this Policy at any time. The version posted on our website at the time of your order will usually apply unless a different written agreement says otherwise.

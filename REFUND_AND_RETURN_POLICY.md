Please read this policy alongside those Terms. If we both accept a written agreement for your order, it takes priority on the topics it specifically covers. A separate written warranty takes priority for its coverage and claim process.

**Your legal rights still apply.** A final-sale label, return deadline, or requirement to get approval does not take away a remedy the law requires for a defect, missing delivery, or other problem. This includes any cancellation or refund rights that apply to international service customers.

## 1. How Returns Work

Return options depend on what you bought. A return simply because you changed your mind needs our approval, unless your written agreement or the law says otherwise. Problems with an item, project cancellations, and warranty claims have their own sections below.

**The default return-request window and Limited Hardware Warranty period are both 14 calendar days after delivery.** Return eligibility and warranty coverage are different, as explained below. Any longer rights required by law still apply.

**Standard hardware** is hardware we regularly stock and do not make to order or customize for a customer. Hardware built for an order, even to an existing design, and hardware designed or modified to your requirements are not standard hardware. We identify made-to-order or custom status in the listing or quote before payment.

Deadlines use **America/Chicago time** and end at **11:59 p.m.** Calendar days include weekends and holidays, with the day after delivery counted as day 1. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding observed US federal holidays, counted from the first business day after the event. A request successfully sent to our listed email address or Discord account on time counts even if we reply later. The full [deadline rules](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md#how-we-count-deadlines) explain message timestamps and return send-by dates; legally required rules take priority.

## 2. Standard Hardware Returns

If you changed your mind about standard hardware that isn't custom or altered, please ask for a return within **14 calendar days after delivery**. We consider requests individually; approval is not guaranteed. The item must be unused, complete, and in a condition we can resell.

Please contact us before sending anything back. If we approve the return, we'll give you the address, a date to send it by, packing instructions, and an explanation of any costs deducted from your refund.

Where the law allows, deductions may include the restocking fee below, original shipping costs, payment fees we cannot recover and have told you about, or documented loss in value from damage or missing parts. We won't charge twice for the same loss or deduct more than the amount otherwise due to you.

## 3. What Usually Cannot Be Returned

Unless we agree otherwise in writing, these purchases cannot be returned just because you changed your mind:

- Made-to-order, custom-built, or custom-modified hardware, including OpenShock-related assemblies made to your specifications.
- Items built, flashed, configured, engraved, or otherwise altered specifically for you.
- Delivered software, firmware, downloads, code, scripts, or configuration files.
- Completed consulting, coding, debugging, integration, repair, or other labor.
- Opened, installed, used, or customer-damaged items, and items clearly designated clearance or final sale before purchase.

These limits do not remove a remedy we owe for a defect, incorrect work, an unfulfilled order, a written warranty, or applicable law. If you've already sent an item without approval, please contact us so we can work out the next step; sending it does not automatically qualify it for a refund.

Where those rules apply, we'll provide the required information and obtain the required request or consent. Accepting this policy alone does not waive those rights. ## 5. Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items

If an item arrives damaged, faulty, incomplete, or significantly different from your order, please get in touch as soon as you can. Report a fault within the **14-calendar-day warranty period after delivery**, unless your order specifies a different period before purchase. Any longer warranty or legal rights still apply, including rights concerning defects discovered later.

Include your order number, a description of the problem, and photos or video if reasonably available and safe to obtain. Do not keep using a potentially unsafe item to reproduce a problem. Keep relevant packaging if possible for a shipping claim.

We may ask you to try reasonable, safe troubleshooting steps or send the item for inspection so we can understand what went wrong. We will give you an expected assessment time when arranging the return, explain any delay, and resolve covered claims within a reasonable time or any shorter period required by law.

### Exchanges Without Sending the Device Back

**You may ask us about this option, but approval is entirely at our discretion and is not guaranteed. Do not destroy your hardware unless we explicitly approve this option in writing for your specific device and exchange and provide instructions. Asking for it, or reading this policy, is not permission to destroy anything.**

If you choose an approved destruction option, you must use the method and specifications we provide and record the process on video.

Wait for our explicit written approval and instructions before doing anything to destroy the device. We'll provide the destruction method, the parts it applies to, and what the video must clearly show to identify the device and confirm that you followed our instructions. Please send the video privately by email or Discord; you do not need to post it publicly or include unrelated personal information.

**Before authorizing destruction, we will confirm and reserve an available replacement.** Our written approval will identify your order and device, the replacement model and specifications, whether it is new or refurbished, any agreed substitute, the replacement shipping cost and payment arrangements, and the expected dispatch date after video verification and any agreed shipping payment. If we cannot secure a suitable replacement, we will not authorize destruction; we'll discuss the normal return process or another available remedy instead.

Once we verify that the video meets the agreed requirements, we'll proceed with the approved exchange without requiring the hardware back. Destruction carried out under our written instructions will not itself disqualify that exchange. Destroying a device without our prior approval does not qualify it for this option.

If we later cannot supply the promised replacement after you followed our approved destruction instructions, we'll offer an alternative you accept or refund the device price and applicable taxes, plus any shipping refund required by law. We will not require you to return hardware we authorized you to destroy. This addresses our failure to complete an approved exchange; it does not make destruction an option for requesting a refund.

Only proceed if you want to use this option and can follow the instructions safely. Do not attempt a method that could injure someone. You can choose the normal return process for any reason, including if you are unsure or cannot safely record the process. Contact us for return instructions before mailing the device. If you choose destruction, dispose of the remains through an appropriate electronics disposal service.

This option does not change exchange eligibility or the exclusion for made-to-order and custom hardware. When we waive the return, there is no return shipping to pay, but you still pay replacement shipping unless a written warranty or applicable law requires otherwise. Shipping charges already paid remain subject to Section 8. Your warranty and legal rights still apply.

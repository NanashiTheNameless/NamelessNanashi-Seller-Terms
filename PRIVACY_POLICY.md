# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what **NamelessNanashi** ("we," "us," "our") does with the information you share when buying from us, getting support, or contacting us. Physical orders are US-only; coding, consulting, and other remotely delivered services are available internationally, where legally permitted. All customers must be at least 18 and meet the age requirement in our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

**We never sell user data for any reason. We do not conduct advertising or marketing, send marketing emails, or share user data for advertising or marketing purposes.**

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on your order and how you contact us, we may receive:

- **Contact information:** your name, email address, shipping and billing addresses, phone number, and Discord username or user ID.
- **Order and transaction information:** quotes, items purchased, invoices, payment status, transaction references, and refund or dispute records.
- **Communications and project materials:** Discord messages, emails, support requests, files, code, specifications, photographs, and other information you send us.

Please send only information needed for your order or request. Do not send passwords, private keys, full payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive information through Discord or email.

## 2. Where Information Is Stored

Customer information and business records are stored through **Stripe, Discord, and email**:

- **Stripe** processes payments and holds payment and transaction records. Payment details you submit to Stripe are handled by Stripe; we receive the transaction and customer information available to us for managing your order.
- **Discord** holds messages and files exchanged with us through Discord.
- **Email** holds correspondence, attachments, and order or support records sent or received by email.

These providers operate their own systems and may collect technical information, such as device, connection, or usage information, when you use their services. Their own processing is subject to their policies. See the [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) and [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). Email providers also apply their own policies to the messages they handle.

Using these services does not authorize us to sell your data or use it for marketing.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

- Prepare quotes, process payments, fulfill orders, and handle cancellations, returns, and refunds.
- Perform agreed custom work, repairs, and other services.
- Answer questions and provide technical or order support.
- Send order confirmations, shipping updates, safety notices, and other messages related to your purchase or request.
- Maintain business records, address fraud or security issues, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

We do not use personal information to send marketing emails, target advertisements, or build marketing profiles.

## 4. When We Share Information

We never sell user data for money or anything else of value. We do not share it for marketing or targeted advertising, including advertising based on your activity across different websites or apps.

Sometimes we need to share information to complete your order or meet our responsibilities. This may include:

- **Stripe, Discord, and email providers** to process transactions, communicate with you, and maintain the records described above.
- **Shipping carriers and contractors** to deliver your order or perform agreed work.
- **Professional advisors** who need it for accounting, legal advice, or related business obligations.
- **Authorities or parties to a legal matter** when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or as reasonably necessary to address fraud, safety, security, or legal claims.

We limit disclosures to information needed for the relevant purpose. Providers may also have independent legal obligations and privacy practices.

That promise also applies if the business is sold: user data will not be an asset we sell.

## 5. Cookies and Provider Settings

We do not use advertising or marketing cookies or tracking technologies. Stripe and Discord may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their own services. Their policies and account settings explain their practices and available choices.

Browser settings may let you block or remove cookies, but doing so can affect payment or communication features. Our no-advertising commitment describes our activities; it is not a claim that independent platforms never conduct their own advertising.

## 6. How Long We Keep Information

**We retain customer information indefinitely through Stripe, Discord, and email.** We do not have a routine deletion schedule for order records, transaction records, messages, or project materials.

The law and our providers' storage and deletion controls still apply. We cannot guarantee that a provider will keep every record forever. If the law requires us to delete information or keep it for a shorter period, we will do so.

You can contact us to ask for deletion. We'll review your request, explain what we can delete and what we need to keep, and carry out any deletion required by law. For example, some tax, payment, or dispute records may need to be kept. Deleting our copy does not necessarily delete records a provider keeps independently under its own obligations.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards for the accounts and information under our control and rely on our providers for the security of their systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 8. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

Contact [Nanashi@NamelessNanashi.dev](mailto:Nanashi@NamelessNanashi.dev) to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Depending on where you live and whether a law applies to our business, you may also have rights to obtain a copy, restrict certain uses, appeal a request decision, or act through an authorized agent.

We may request information reasonably needed to verify your identity or an agent's authority. Do not send identity documents unless we request them through an appropriate process. We will respond within any deadline required by applicable law and explain if we cannot fulfill all or part of a request. You do not need to opt out of data sales or advertising: we do not sell your information or share it for those purposes in the first place.

## 9. Adults Only - 18 and Over

Our products and services are intended for adults. **We do not sell products or provide services to anyone under 18.** Customers must also meet any higher age-of-majority requirement described in our Terms of Service.

If you're under 18, please do not place an order, hire us, or send us personal information. If you believe someone under 18 has provided personal information, please contact us. We'll review the situation, stop any ineligible order or service engagement, and handle the information as required by law, including deletion where required. Any cancellation or refund will follow applicable law and our [Refund and Return Policy](REFUND_AND_RETURN_POLICY.md).

We are based in the United States and accept international customers for remotely delivered services. Your information may be handled in the United States or other countries where our providers operate. Their policies explain their processing locations and international transfers. The US-only restriction on physical orders does not mean that all information is stored only in the US.

Links to third-party services do not make their privacy practices part of ours. Review their policies before providing information directly to them.

We may update this Privacy Policy and will identify the effective date of each version.
